## What has happened

IBPS notified CRP PO/MT-XVI on 1 July 2026 and CRP CSA-XVI (Clerk) on 1 August 2026. Both application windows were extended once: PO from 21 to 26 July, Clerk from 21 to 28 August. Clerk then got a two-day edit window, 4 to 5 September, which has also closed.

PO vacancies moved twice after the original notification: 6,715 indicative posts on 1 July, revised to 7,365 on 20 July, then to 7,565 on 27 August after UCO Bank added 200 posts. Clerk/CSA vacancies have stayed at 11,403 since the original Annexure I and have not been revised.

## What changed this year

The clerical post is now officially Customer Service Associate (CSA), not Clerk, though the exam and process are unchanged. Both notifications carry a mandatory live photo capture step in addition to the usual scanned upload, done through your browser's webcam or by scanning a QR code with your phone. IBPS Clerk Mains also picked up five extra Quantitative Aptitude questions this cycle (160 questions for 200 marks, up from 155).

## How to apply

- PO and Clerk are separate applications with separate fees; there's no combined form.
- Register fresh on ibps.in for each exam (no permanent OTR); you get a provisional registration number and password.
- Upload photo, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration in one sitting, then pay online by card, net banking or UPI.
- Clerk additionally asks for your State/UT preference and proof of local language proficiency.

## Photo and signature

These are the CSA-XVI Annexure IV specifications, read from the official notification. IBPS normally uses the same sheet across its CRP cycles, so PO candidates should still check their own notification.

- **Photo**: 4.5 × 3.5 cm, 200×230 px preferred, 20 to 50 KB, JPG/JPEG, colour, white or light background, no cap or dark glasses. A live capture is also mandatory.
- **Signature**: black ink on white paper, 140×60 px preferred, 10 to 20 KB, not in capital letters.
- **Left thumb impression**: black or blue ink on white paper, 240×240 px at 200 DPI, 20 to 50 KB. If your left thumb is unavailable, the notification specifies a fallback order (right thumb, then fingers, then toe).
- **Handwritten declaration**: English only, your own handwriting, black ink on white paper, 800×400 px at 200 DPI, 50 to 100 KB, not in capital letters.

## Before you upload

- Scan at 200 DPI in true colour, then crop to the pixel dimensions above before you compress
- Sign and write the declaration in running hand, IBPS rejects capital letters on both
- Keep your left thumb impression on plain white paper, not lined or coloured
- Copy the declaration text exactly, word for word, including your own name in place of the blank
- Have your SSC/10th certificate scanned as a PDF, A4 page size, under 500 KB, ready before you start
