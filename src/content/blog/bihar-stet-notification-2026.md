## What has happened

Bihar School Examination Board released Bihar STET 2026 notification P.R. 183/2026 on 14 August 2026, following an Education Department letter dated 11 August 2026. Applications opened 17 August and close 31 August 2026, the deadline you're reading this against.

STET is a teacher eligibility test for Classes 9 to 12, not a recruitment against fixed posts, so there is no vacancy number attached to this cycle. Clearing it gives a certificate valid for life, which candidates later use in separate teacher recruitment drives.

## What changed this year

This cycle was notified and closed inside three weeks, in mid to late August, well ahead of STET 2025's September to October window. Fee, qualifying marks and age brackets carry over unchanged from 2025.

No official applicant count has been published for 2026 yet. For comparison, STET 2025 had 4,42,214 candidates appear (2,46,415 in Paper I, 1,95,799 in Paper II), and STET 2024 had 4,23,822 appear across both papers.

## How to apply

- Register on the portal with a mobile number and email OTP; there's no separate one-time registration system, this login is created fresh for STET 2026.
- Fill the form, upload your photo and signature, then pay online by debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI. No other payment mode is accepted.
- Apply for Paper I, Paper II, or both; fee and eligibility are checked separately for each paper.

## Photo and signature

The photo is a recent colour photograph, roughly 3.5 x 4.5 cm, on a white or light background, uploaded as JPG/JPEG, not captured live through a webcam. [UNVERIFIED: exact file-size limit, sources conflict between 20 to 100 KB and 80 to 200 KB, check the live upload screen before submitting]

The signature is scanned, written by hand in running script rather than block or capital letters, black ink on white paper, JPG/JPEG. [UNVERIFIED: exact file-size limit, sources conflict between 10 to 50 KB and 50 to 200 KB]

Full specs and history for this exam sit on the [Bihar STET exam page](/exams/bihar-stet/). If your files don't fit, resize them free with the [photo resizer](/tools/photo-resizer/) before you upload.

## Before you upload

- Scan on white paper only, no ruled lines or watermark showing through
- Sign in running handwriting, BSEB explicitly rejects block or capital letters
- Use a photo taken in the last few months, plain light background, no cap or dark glasses
- Save both files as JPG or JPEG before attempting upload
- Keep a second correctly sized copy ready in case the portal rejects the first attempt
